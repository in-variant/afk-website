Questions

What is Runs?

Each person on your team gets a capy that learns from their own work: email, calendar, documents and tasks. Teammates ask it questions and get answers without waiting for that person.

What does it capture?

Only the tools you connect. You can pause capture and remove anything at any time.

Who can see my capy?

Only people signed in with your company's domain. Every answer shows its sources, so you can see what was used.

How long does setup take?

About ten minutes per person. We set it up with your team on a call.

What happens when someone leaves?

Their capy keeps what they knew, so the next person can ask it what was done and where it is.

Which tools does it work with?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Slack today. Tell us what else your team uses.
