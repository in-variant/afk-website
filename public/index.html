FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Runs?

Runs gives each person on your team a capy: a helper that remembers their work from email, calendar, documents and tasks, and answers teammates for them.

What does my capy see?

Only the tools you connect. You can pause it whenever you like and delete anything it has kept.

Can my manager see what I do?

No. Your capy answers questions about your work when a teammate asks. There are no activity reports or screen time dashboards.

Who can ask my capy questions?

Only people signed in with your company's domain. Every answer shows where it came from.

How long does setup take?

About ten minutes per person. We set it up with your team on a call.

What happens when someone leaves?

Their capy keeps what they knew, so the next person can ask what was done, why, and where it is.

Which tools does it work with?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Slack today, with Notion and Linear next. Tell us what else your team uses.
