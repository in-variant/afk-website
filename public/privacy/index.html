Privacy

A memory this personal has to stay private

Your capy works for you. You decide what it sees, what it keeps and what it forgets.

Our principles

Four promises

You choose what it sees

Nothing is connected by default. You turn on each tool yourself, and you can turn it off again.

Pause or delete anytime

Pause capture for an hour or for good, and remove anything your capy has kept.

It works for you

Your capy answers questions about your work when a teammate asks. It does not report on you.

Separate by design

Each capy runs on its own private cloud machine, and only your company's domain can sign in.

In plain words

What Runs does and does not do

Runs does

  • See only the apps and tools you turn on
  • Follow along on screen only when you allow it
  • Keep a memory of your work so it can answer for you
  • Show the source of every answer
  • Let you pause, delete and disconnect at any time
  • Store data on Google Cloud, encrypted in transit and at rest

Runs does not

  • See any app you have switched off
  • Produce activity reports or screen-time dashboards
  • Rank or score people
  • Let anyone outside your company domain sign in
  • Sell your data
Questions

Privacy questions

Can my manager read my capy's memory?

No. Teammates can ask your capy questions about your work, and it answers with sources. Nobody can browse your memory or see activity reports.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Your team decides with you what your capy keeps for handover. Anything you want removed can be deleted.

Where is my data stored?

On a private Google Cloud machine for each person, encrypted in transit and at rest.

Can I stop it from seeing something?

Yes. Disconnect a tool, pause capture, or delete specific items at any time.
