FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Runs?

Runs gives each person on your team a capy: a helper that remembers their work from email, calendar, documents, meetings and tasks, and answers teammates for them.

How long does setup take?

About ten minutes per person. We set it up with your team on a call.

Can I mix plans?

Yes. Some people can be on Starter while the rest of the team is on Team. Talk to us and we will set it up.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are monthly, and you can cancel or change them at any time.

Which tools does it work with?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, meeting notes and Slack today, with Notion and Linear next.

Can my manager see what I do?

No. Your capy answers questions about your work when a teammate asks. There are no activity reports.
