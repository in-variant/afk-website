    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    What is AFK?

    AFK gives each person on your team a Kip: a helper that remembers their work from email, calendar, documents, meetings and tasks, and answers teammates for them.

    What does my Kip see?

    Only the tools you connect. You can pause it whenever you like and delete anything it has kept.

    Can my manager see what I do?

    No. Your Kip answers questions about your work when a teammate asks. There are no activity reports or screen time dashboards.

    Who can ask my Kip questions?

    Only people signed in with your company's domain. Every answer shows where it came from.

    How long does setup take?

    About ten minutes per person. We set it up with your team on a call.

    What happens when someone leaves?

    Their Kip keeps what they knew, so the next person can ask what was done, why, and where it is.

    Which tools does it work with?

    Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, meeting notes and Slack today, with Notion and Linear next. Tell us what else your team uses.
